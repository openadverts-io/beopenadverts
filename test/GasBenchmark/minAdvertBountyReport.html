OpenAdverts — Minimum advertBounty Guidance

Generated 2026-09-02T14:24:15.747Z · POL advert · 20-sig batch · viewer 70% · 3-TP unique · gas/sig 90.297 (amortised over 20 sigs)

Model & scope.
  • Both the payout and the gas are in POL, so the viewer's gas-breakeven advertBounty is invariant to the POL/USD price — price only scales the magnitude. USD price matters only when comparing the advertiser's cost to Google (a USD benchmark).
  • Costs are amortised over a 20-signature batch (never a single signature). The viewer pays 1.805.940 gas once for 20 engagements; 90.297 is the per-engagement share.
  • Viewer receives 70% of the bounty; all third-party addresses unique (worst-case gas path). Gas is split-ratio-invariant (same recipients at 50% or 70%).
  • Google benchmarks are editable constants in the script — currently CPV $0.010000/view, CPC $0.100000/click, CPM $0.000500/impression.
